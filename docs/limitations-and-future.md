# Limitations and Future Enhancements

## Current limitations

- Qualification Assessments are optional.
- Copilot recommendations are advisory only.
- Copilot cannot record the official Bid / No Bid decision.
- Multi-user security testing has not been performed.
- Cross-environment deployment testing is deferred.

## Possible future enhancements

- Reviewer and Decision Maker security personas
- Column-level protection of official decision fields
- Formal approval workflow if required by business governance
- Stored AI qualification assessments
- Teams or Microsoft 365 Copilot deployment
- Automated deployment pipelines
