# Deterministic Qualification Scoring

Tender qualification is calculated deterministically rather than by generative AI.

Scoring considers:

- CPV alignment
- Capability keyword matches
- Geography
- Submission deadline

Fit Bands:

- High: 70+
- Medium: 45–69
- Low: 44 or below

The Baseline Fit Score and stored score explanation are authoritative.

Copilot Studio may interpret the result but does not recalculate or override it.
