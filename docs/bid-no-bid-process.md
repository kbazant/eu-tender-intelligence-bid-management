# Bid / No Bid Process

Tender lifecycle:

New → Awaiting Review → Ready for Decision → Bid / No Bid

The model-driven EU Tender Bid Manager is used to review the opportunity and record the final decision.

The official decision is made by an authorized human user.

When a Bid / No Bid decision is recorded, the solution requires:

- Decision Reason
- Decision Date
- Decision By

Copilot Studio may provide a non-binding recommendation but cannot record the official decision.
